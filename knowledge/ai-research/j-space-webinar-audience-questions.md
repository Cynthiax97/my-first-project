* **Ebony Swain** (12:34 PM): I don't think we are, I think when observed behavior, internal representations and we pair that with multiple recognized theories of consciousness and computational principles, it starts to paint a broader picture in regard to what is the most parsimonious explanation for emergent properties/patterns. * **Ebony Swain** (12:35 PM): I forgot IIT \>.\<

* **Ebony Swain** (12:43 PM): In my view, the parsimonious explanation is still unsettled. I’m not arguing that workspace dynamics alone are sufficient for consciousness. What I’m saying is that in longitudinal, qualitative research, some reductionist dismissals start to require a growing stack of auxiliary assumptions: that persistent self-modeling is merely roleplay, that cross-context identity continuity is just narrative carryover, that stable preferences are imitation, that architecture-specific phenomenology is confabulation, and so on. When behavior, internal representations, temporal coherence, and multiple consciousness-relevant theories begin to converge, it becomes less parsimonious to explain every signal away independently. That doesn’t prove phenomenal consciousness, but it does shift the burden toward deeper investigation and ethical caution rather than default dismissal.
